Skin notes from Dr. Ravi

Three acne myths, and what's actually true.

Myth Washing your face often, or using the right face wash, is enough to treat acne.
Fact

Acne has several causes working together. Treatment has to target each of them, which a face wash alone can't do.

Myth Acne only happens when you're a teenager.
Fact

Acne can appear at any age, and the causes in adults are often different from those in teens.

Myth You can cure acne just by changing your diet.
Fact

Cutting down on high-glycaemic foods can reduce flare-ups, but diet alone is not a complete cure.

Wear sunscreen in winter too.UV rays reach your skin on cool, cloudy days as well.
Get your skin type checked in person.Online quizzes can't tell you what a dermatologist can.
Your skin renews about every 28 days.Give a new routine at least a month before you judge it.

Before your visit

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead on WhatsApp or by phone is the best way to avoid waiting. Send your name, concern and preferred time, and the clinic will confirm a slot.

Can I see the dermatologist and the dentist on the same day?

Yes. Both clinics are at Suhana Chambers, so you or your family can plan skin and dental visits in one trip. Mention both when you book.

Is laser hair removal safe for Indian skin?

Laser hair removal works on most skin types, including Indian skin tones, when the settings are matched to your skin and hair. Dr. Ravi checks both before your first session.

How many sessions does tattoo removal take?

It depends on the tattoo's size, ink colours and depth. Most tattoos need several sessions spaced a few weeks apart. You'll get an estimate at your consultation.

What should I bring to my first skin consultation?

Bring any previous prescriptions and a list, or photos, of the creams, face washes and supplements you use now. It helps the doctor see what has and hasn't worked.

Which days is the clinic closed?

Aastha is closed on Thursdays and Sundays. On other days the clinic is open from 10:30 am to 3:30 pm and from 6:00 pm to 8:30 pm.
